Where to find your calendar's link

Most calendar apps have a "secret address," "iCal link," or "public URL" for a calendar under its sharing or settings page — Google Calendar calls it the "Secret address in iCal format," Apple Calendar and Outlook call it a "Public Calendar" link. Paste that link above. Only calendars with this kind of link (webcal:// or a URL ending in .ics) can be added.

Default view

What the display shows when it first loads (e.g. after a restart or refresh) — not a restriction on switching views afterward. Layout only applies to Week and 3-Day; Month always shows the grid. This is per-installation, so each display can be set to what that household prefers.

Timeline view hours

Controls the Week/3-Day Timeline layout only (not Stacked). Hours are 0–24, where 0 is 12 AM and 24 is 12 AM the next day — for example, 5 and 22 shows 5 AM–10 PM, while 0 and 24 shows the full day. Default is 4 and 23 (4 AM–11 PM).
